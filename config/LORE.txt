========================================
HAWAK KO ANG BIT: THE FINAL BIT
SYSTEM LORE ARCHIVE
========================================

YEAR: UNKNOWN

The system was once stable.

Cities, communication networks, digital archives,
security infrastructures, and artificial systems
were all connected to a single global computational core.

This core was known as:

THE CENTRAL SYSTEM.

At the center of the system existed a tiny but essential fragment
known only as:

THE BIT.

The Bit was never meant to control the system.
It was designed to maintain balance.

A single unit.
A single signal.
A single surviving instruction.

But then...

the collapse began.

Unknown malicious entities infiltrated the network.
Viruses corrupted memory sectors.
Worms consumed communication lines.
Trojans infiltrated protected cores.

Entire sectors of the digital infrastructure failed.

The Boot Sector was the first to collapse.

Then the Memory Core followed.

Eventually, the System Kernel itself became unstable.

As the corruption spread, the Central System initiated
its final emergency protocol.

PROJECT: FINAL BIT.

The Bit was awakened.

Not as a weapon.

But as the system's final surviving process.

Your mission is to travel through the collapsing digital sectors,
eliminate the corruption,
restore the damaged systems,
and reach the core before total shutdown occurs.

If the Kernel collapses...

the entire system disappears forever.

You are the final process still running.
